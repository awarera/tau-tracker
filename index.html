What is TAU Tracker?
TAU Trade (tau-trade.com) is one of Japan's largest damaged-vehicle auction platforms — over 1,000 cars listed daily from crashes, floods, and write-offs. TAU Tracker pulls those listings every morning, enriches them with live exchange rates, and gives you a single workspace to identify, evaluate, and plan import deals from Nairobi.
Data updates daily at 08:00 EAT. Hit Sync (top right) for a manual refresh. Prices shown in ¥ and USD. KES conversions use live rates.
▲ ROUTE A Fix & Export
Buy a lightly damaged, rolling car at auction. Repair it at a Japanese bodyshop (typically ¥50K–¥300K). Ship it to Mombasa as a single unit. Clear duty and sell in Kenya at significantly above landed cost.
Illustrative example
TAU bid price¥350,000 ($2,240) Japan repair¥120,000 ($770) Shipping + CIF$1,100 Kenya duty + clearingKES 220,000 Total landed cost≈ KES 800,000 Kenya resale≈ KES 1,500,000
Margin ≈ KES 700,000
⚙ ROUTE B Parts Harvest
Buy a heavily damaged car — not rolling. Dismantle it in Japan: engine, gearbox, body panels, hybrid battery. Load a 20ft container and ship to Mombasa. Sell individual parts through Peach's existing network.
Illustrative example
TAU bid price (x8 cars)¥800,000 ($5,100) Dismantling labour¥200,000 ($1,280) 20ft container freight$3,200 Kenya clearingKES 85,000 Total cost≈ KES 1,500,000 Parts resale value≈ KES 3,200,000
Margin ≈ KES 1,700,000
